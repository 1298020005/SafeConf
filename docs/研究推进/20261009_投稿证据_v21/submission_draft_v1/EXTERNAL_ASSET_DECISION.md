# External asset decision after the KOLF evaluation

This is a metadata-only audit. It does not reopen any sealed truth or change the frozen SafeConf results.

## Decision

Do not start a second external experiment by default. The available local candidates do not currently provide a clean second qualified replication under the same PublicRule contract. KOLF remains the single qualified frozen independent evaluation, while the other assets are reported in their existing roles.

## Candidate screening

| Asset | Available evidence | Decision | Reason |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Adamson panels | 48 gene clusters, 96 predictor records | Seen replication only | Already used with Replogle GWPS source history; small panel and not a new confirmation contract |
| E177 Sunshine | 400 hidden evaluation tasks | Stress/certificate evidence only | External ranking correlations were about 0.057; it supports the deterministic certificate more than risk ranking |
| E190 Adamson→Replogle | 692 tasks, 47 common perturbation clusters | Not SafeConf confirmation without a new contract | Existing experiment evaluates direct transfer and divergence certificates, not the frozen PublicRule risk interface |
| E192 Adamson→Replogle RPE1 | 175 tasks, 21 gene clusters | Do not promote | The registered empirical ranking gate abstained; the effective biological sample is too small for a stronger independent claim |
| KOLF2.1J | 300 frozen evaluation genes, one predictor passed competence gate | Adopted | PublicRule scores and truth were frozen under the current contract; the interval is broad and is reported as such |

## Principal investigator decision

The marginal value of another unqualified or contract-incompatible dataset is lower than the risk of expanding the study after seeing the current results. The manuscript will therefore emphasize the reproducible cold-start protocol, the strong McFaline result, the Frangieh stress boundary, and the qualified but uncertain KOLF evaluation. A second external study can be added only if a future metadata audit finds a predictor, public-memory exclusion, response contract, and independent cluster count that all pass before truth access.
